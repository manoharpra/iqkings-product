## What LedgerKing does

LedgerKing keeps the complete books of a shop, trader, small business or accountant on your own Windows PC:

- **Accounting**: Contra, Payment, Receipt, Journal, Sales and Purchase (F4–F9), credit and debit notes, Day Book,
  Ledger, Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Outstanding with ageing.
- **Bills and stock**: GST invoices with live tax, stock items, batches, locations (godowns), FIFO stock value,
  quotations, sales and purchase orders, delivery challans, POS counter billing with barcodes.
- **GST**: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B JSON for the GST portal, GSTR-2B matching with purchase bills, e-invoice and e-way
  bill JSON, RCM, ITC register, composition and unregistered dealers, exports and SEZ.
- **More**: TDS / TCS, bank reconciliation, cheque printing, cost centres, budgets, foreign currency, payment
  reminders on WhatsApp / e-mail, recurring bills (rent, salary, EMI), month-end party statements.
- **Grow with it**: party-wise **price lists** (wholesale, retail, dealer), a **fixed asset register** with
  depreciation (Income-tax WDV or straight line, sale / scrap with profit or loss) and **manufacturing** with a
  bill of material, shortage check and production cost.
- **Move in easily**: import from Tally (XML), Excel / CSV, and Busy / Marg exports.

## System requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit). WebView2 is part of Windows; the installer adds it if missing.
- Installs for the current user (no administrator rights needed).
- Data is kept in `Documents\LedgerKing` on a **local disk**. Network drives and live OneDrive / Dropbox folders
  are not used for the data file (backups may go anywhere).
