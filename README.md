# IQKings Products

Business software made in India, for Indian businesses. Every IQKings app works **offline on your own
computer**, follows Indian GST rules and is sold with simple plans on **[www.iqkings.com](https://www.iqkings.com)**.

This repository is the public information page for IQKings products: what each app does, how it keeps your
data safe, what changed in each version and what comes next. The source code of the apps is private.

## Buying, licenses and support

- **Download and buy** only on [www.iqkings.com](https://www.iqkings.com). Payment is by UPI or bank transfer;
  the license is issued to your account and works offline.
- **Support**: Customer Support in your account on iqkings.com, or the chat on the website.
- **Guides**: step-by-step help (GSTR-1 JSON, e-way bill, moving from Tally) at
  [iqkings.com/guides](https://www.iqkings.com/guides).

## Found a problem or want a feature?

Open an issue in this repository with the **Bug report** or **Feature request** form, or write to support
from your account. For a security problem, read [ledgerking/SECURITY.md](ledgerking/SECURITY.md) first and do
not post details in a public issue.
